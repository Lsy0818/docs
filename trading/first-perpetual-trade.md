# Your First Perpetual Trade

Perpetual futures are leveraged contracts without an expiry date. They allow traders to take long or short exposure without exchanging the underlying asset directly.

## Open a position

1. Connect Rocky Wallet, Loop Wallet, or Console Wallet.
2. Select a perpetual market from the market selector.
3. Choose **Long** if you expect the market price to rise, or **Short** if you expect it to fall.
4. Select an available order type.
5. Enter the order size and review the required margin.
6. Set leverage within the market's permitted range.
7. Review the estimated entry price, fees, liquidation price, and other order details.
8. Submit the order and approve the required wallet action.
9. Monitor the order or position from the relevant account panel.

## Manage or close a position

After a position is open, monitor:

- Position size
- Entry price
- Mark price
- Unrealized profit and loss
- Margin usage
- Liquidation price
- Funding rate

To exit, submit a closing order for all or part of the position. A position is not closed until the closing order is filled and the application reflects the updated position.

> **Risk warning**
>
> Leverage amplifies both gains and losses. A relatively small adverse price move can result in liquidation and the loss of the margin allocated to a position.
